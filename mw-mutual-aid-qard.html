💵 Interest-Free Loan (Qard)

A simple temporary money loan for a genuine need, with repayment of the agreed principal and no interest or hidden profit.

What this service entails

State the genuine need

The borrower briefly explains the purpose without exposing unnecessary private information.

Agree the principal

Both sides record the exact amount being lent so there is no disagreement about what must be repaid.

Agree a repayment plan

A realistic date or simple instalment plan can be agreed according to the borrower’s circumstances.

Record the agreement

The amount, date and repayment terms are written down so both sides keep the same understanding.

Private hand-off

Contact details are shared only after verification and mutual acceptance; Muslim Welfare does not receive or transmit the money.

Confirm repayment

When the agreed principal is repaid, both sides can confirm completion.

Important safeguards

No interest. The lender should not require a percentage, extra payment or hidden profit merely because of the loan.
No public shame. Repayment difficulty should be handled privately and respectfully.
No guarantee. Muslim Welfare can verify and record but cannot guarantee repayment.
