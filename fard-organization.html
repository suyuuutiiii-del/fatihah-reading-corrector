Institutional Verification

Mosques and recognised Islamic organisations can become local FARD verifiers. Their role is to confirm identity or genuine community context — not to expose private information or automatically certify specialist skills.

🕌 Local trust

A mosque may know whether a family, volunteer or need is genuinely connected to its community.

🛡️ Limited authority

Organisation verification grants Community Verified status. Specialist skill verification remains a separate higher standard.

🔐 Private keys

Approved verifier organisations use a private verifier key. The key must never be published or shared casually.
