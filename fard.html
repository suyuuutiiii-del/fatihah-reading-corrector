How FARD works

1
Need or offer
A Muslim, mosque or organisation submits a need or useful ability.
2
Verify
A mosque, organisation or FARD administrator checks the entry.
3
Match
Service, area, availability, language, task and safety requirements are compared.
4
Mutual consent
Both sides accept before private contact details are revealed.
5
Serve
The task is carried out within the verified scope and safety boundaries.
6
Confirm & learn
Both sides confirm completion. Recurring needs can return to the matching pool.

Verification levels

Pending
Submitted but not yet ready for matching.
Community Verified
A recognised mosque, organisation or community verifier confirms the person/request.
Skill Verified
Where competence matters, a claimed specialist skill has suitable supporting verification.
Completed Service
A previous match was completed and confirmed; useful trust history, but not a guarantee.

FARD Trust & Safety

🔐 Privacy first
Exact addresses and private contact details are withheld until mutual acceptance.
🧒 Safeguarding
Children are not privately matched with unrelated adults. Guardians or responsible institutions remain involved.
💳 No exploitation
A service match must not become pressure for donations, loans, sales or unrelated commitments.
🩺 Professional limits
FARD community service never replaces emergency, medical, legal or regulated professional care.
⏸ Safety hold
A serious incident can place a match on hold while an administrator reviews it.
🤝 Mutual consent
Private contact is revealed only after requester and volunteer both accept.

Report a concern

Immediate danger: do not wait for FARD moderation if there is immediate danger, serious injury, abuse, violence, fire or active crime. Contact the appropriate local emergency, healthcare or security service and a trusted local institution.
