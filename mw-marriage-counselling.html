🫶 Marriage Counselling

Structured help for spouses who want to improve communication, cooperation and family stability.

What this service entails

Understand the real problem

The counsellor helps separate the immediate argument from deeper recurring issues such as communication, finances, trust or family pressure.

Give both spouses a voice

Each spouse should be able to explain concerns without humiliation, intimidation or one-sided domination of the session.

Improve communication

The couple can practise listening, calmer disagreement and clearer requests instead of repeating accusations.

Agree practical changes

Rather than vague promises, the couple identifies a few specific behaviours or arrangements to change.

Review progress

A follow-up session can check what improved, what failed and whether additional support is needed.

Refer beyond counselling when needed

Medical, legal, safeguarding, addiction or complex religious issues should be referred to appropriate specialists.

Important: Counselling should not be used to pressure a person to remain in an unsafe or abusive situation.
