Practical Care

A simple way for a Muslim household to ask for practical support — and for trustworthy Muslims nearby to offer time, transport, meals, errands and compassionate presence.

What Practical Care can cover

How a care request should work

1
Request
Describe the practical task and area without publishing an exact home address.
2
Verify
A mosque, recognised Islamic organisation or FARD moderator checks that the request is genuine.
3
Match
A suitable volunteer is selected by task, area, availability and safety requirements.
4
Safe hand-off
Private contact details and precise location are shared only after verification and acceptance.
5
Completion check
The requester or verifier confirms that the support was completed appropriately.

Practical Care safety rules

Protect the exact address
Only town/area is used during the public or moderation stage. Precise location belongs in the verified hand-off stage.
No unsupervised minor matching
Volunteers are not privately matched with children. A parent, guardian, mosque or recognised organisation must remain responsible.
No medical substitution
Hospital accompaniment and medicine pickup may be practical tasks; clinical care and medication decisions belong to qualified professionals.
No pressure for money
Volunteers must not turn a care match into an unverified fundraising request, sale or loan arrangement.
Respect household boundaries
Doorstep or public-point delivery should be used when a home visit is unnecessary. Home visits should follow the household's safe arrangement.
Report and stop
If a match becomes unsafe, inappropriate or materially different from the verified request, either party may stop and report it.
