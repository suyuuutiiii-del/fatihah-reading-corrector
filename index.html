Read the complete Bismillah naturally.

What this first test focuses on
  • اللَّهِ: the lām is light because it follows a kasrah.
  • الرَّحْمَٰنِ: do not pronounce the lām of ال; give the رّ its shaddah; keep the natural madd at مَٰ.
  • الرَّحِيمِ: give the رّ its shaddah; keep the natural madd at حِي.
