STAGE 1 • BISMILLAH
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Read the complete Bismillah naturally.

Practice focus
  • اللَّهِ: the lām is light because it follows a kasrah.
  • الرَّحْمَٰنِ: the lām of ال is assimilated into رّ; give the rā its shaddah and keep the natural madd at مَٰ.
  • الرَّحِيمِ: give رّ its shaddah and keep the natural madd at حِي.

STAGE 2 • ĀYAH 2
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds.

What this test focuses on
  • الْحَمْدُ: pronounce the lām of الْ clearly; do not swallow it.
  • لِلَّهِ: the lām in the name of Allah is light because it is preceded by kasrah.
  • رَبِّ: give the بّ its shaddah; do not read it as a single bā.
  • الْعَالَمِينَ: pronounce the lām of الْ clearly and keep the natural madd in عَا for about two counts. If stopping at the end, the final madd in مِينْ may be 2, 4, or 6 counts.
