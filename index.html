Expert example

  1. Listen carefully to the expert recitation.
  2. Record your own recitation.
  3. Play both recordings and compare each word.
  4. Ask a qualified teacher about tajwīd or pronunciation differences.

Live mode checks recognised words. Record / Upload allows careful comparison but does not automatically judge tajwīd. A qualified teacher is needed for detailed makhārij and tajwīd correction.
