🕊️ Janāzah Support

Help a bereaved Muslim family quickly find verified people for the practical communal duties around death, funeral preparation and burial.

What this service can cover

🧼 Ghusl team

Find suitable trained Muslims to assist with washing and preparation.

🧵 Kafan & preparation

Coordinate shrouding supplies and people familiar with the process.

🕌 Prayer coordination

Connect the family with a mosque or imam for the funeral prayer.

⚰️ Burial coordination

Help coordinate cemetery, transport and practical burial arrangements.

🍲 Family support

Meals, errands and practical support for the bereaved household.

📋 Coordination

Bring the family, mosque and verified service volunteers into one clear plan.

How FARD handles a janazah request

1
Describe
State the need, general area and timing without publishing sensitive details.
2
Verify
A mosque, recognised organisation, community verifier or FARD moderator checks the request.
3
Match
A suitable verified volunteer is selected by service, area, availability and safeguards.
4
Complete & confirm
The service is completed and the requester or verifier confirms the outcome.

Janāzah Support safety rules

Qualified people for sensitive duties
Ghusl and other sensitive funeral tasks should be carried out by people who understand the relevant Islamic rulings and local requirements.
Respect sex and privacy boundaries
Washing and preparation must respect Islamic privacy and appropriate same-sex arrangements, except where a recognised ruling permits otherwise.
Confirm legal/medical release
FARD does not bypass required medical, legal, coroner or registration procedures.
No exploitation of bereaved families
No volunteer may pressure a grieving family for money, sales, loans or unrelated commitments.
Protect private information
Do not publish cause-of-death details, identity documents or exact family addresses.
Scholar/mosque escalation
Unusual religious questions should be referred to a qualified scholar or the responsible mosque.
