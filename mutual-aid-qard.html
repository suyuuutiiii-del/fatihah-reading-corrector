💵 Interest-Free Loan (Qard)

A simple temporary money loan for a genuine need, with repayment of the agreed principal and no interest or profit to the lender.

What this service entails

State the genuine need

The borrower briefly explains the purpose of the loan without exposing unnecessary private details.

Agree the exact principal

Both sides record the exact amount being lent so there is no later disagreement about what must be repaid.

Agree a repayment plan

The parties may agree a date or simple instalment plan that the borrower reasonably expects to meet.

Record the agreement

FARD can keep a simple acknowledgement of the amount, date and repayment terms so both sides have the same understanding.

Private hand-off

Contact details are revealed only after verification and mutual acceptance. FARD itself does not receive or transmit the loan money.

Confirm repayment

When the agreed principal has been repaid, both sides can confirm completion and close the arrangement.

Protection against misuse

No interest
The lender should not require a percentage, extra payment or hidden profit merely because of the loan.
No coercion
A borrower should not be pressured into unrelated sales, employment, political activity, personal favours or humiliating conditions.
No public debtor list
Late repayment is a private moderation matter, not a reason to expose or shame someone publicly.
Small and simple first
FARD should begin with modest, understandable community loans rather than acting like a commercial lender.
