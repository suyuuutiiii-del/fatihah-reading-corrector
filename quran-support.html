Qur’an Support safety rules

Guardian protection for minors
Children must not be privately matched to unrelated adults without a parent/guardian or responsible institution supervising the arrangement.
Verify teaching claims
Where a teacher claims ijāzah, certification or specialist expertise, FARD should seek appropriate verification before displaying it.
No automatic religious authority
Being a Qur’an volunteer does not make someone a mufti or scholar for unrelated religious rulings.
Appropriate communication
Private messaging and meeting arrangements should respect age, gender, family and safeguarding boundaries.
Learning, not humiliation
Correction should be respectful; public shaming and harsh treatment are not acceptable.
Escalate recitation disputes
Advanced qirāʾāt or technical disputes should be referred to suitably qualified teachers.
