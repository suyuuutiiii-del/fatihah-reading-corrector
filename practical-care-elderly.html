👴 Elderly Support

Errands, accompaniment, basic household assistance and compassionate presence for older Muslims.

What this service entails

These are the main forms of practical help normally covered under this service.

Groceries and essential errands

A volunteer may shop for agreed essentials or collect needed items for an older Muslim who finds such errands difficult.

Accompaniment

A helper may accompany the person to the mosque, clinic, community programme or another essential destination when appropriate.

Light household assistance

Simple non-clinical tasks may be offered, such as carrying items, changing a light bulb where safe, or helping organise basic household needs.

Friendly check-ins

A trusted volunteer may make agreed visits or calls to reduce isolation and notice practical needs that should be passed to family or responsible carers.

Transport coordination

Where transport is the main need, FARD can connect the elderly person with the verified Transport branch rather than leaving them to arrange it alone.

Safety and boundaries

Verify the need
A mosque, organisation or FARD moderator can confirm the local context before matching.
Protect privacy
Exact address and private contact details stay hidden until a suitable match is accepted.
Keep the role practical
This is community assistance and does not replace emergency, medical, legal or other professional services.
