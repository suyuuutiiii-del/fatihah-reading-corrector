🛡️ Trust & Safety

Safety takes priority over speed, convenience or reputation.

Privacy first
Exact addresses and private contacts stay protected until mutual acceptance.
Safeguarding
Children are not privately matched to unrelated adults.
No exploitation
Matches must not become pressure for money, loans or unrelated sales.
Safety hold
High/critical reports can pause an active match for review.

Report a concern

Immediate danger: do not wait for FARD moderation. Contact the appropriate emergency, healthcare or security service and a trusted local institution.
