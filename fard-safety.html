Trust & Safety Centre

FARD is designed to help Muslims serve one another without making vulnerability public. Safety takes priority over speed, convenience or reputation.

🔐 Privacy first

Exact addresses and private contact details are withheld until an appropriate match has been accepted by both sides.

🧒 Safeguarding

Children are not privately matched with unrelated adults. Guardians or responsible institutions remain involved.

💳 No exploitation

A service match must not become pressure for donations, loans, sales or unrelated financial commitments.

🩺 Professional limits

FARD community service never replaces emergency, medical, legal or other regulated professional care.

⏸ Safety hold

A serious report can place a match on hold while an administrator reviews it.

🤝 Mutual consent

Private contact is revealed only after both requester and volunteer accept the proposed match.

Report a concern about a FARD match

Use the private management key belonging to your request or volunteer profile. High/critical reports may automatically place an active match on safety hold pending review.

Immediate danger: Do not wait for FARD moderation if there is immediate danger, serious injury, abuse, violence, fire or active crime. Contact the appropriate local emergency, healthcare or security service and a trusted local institution.
