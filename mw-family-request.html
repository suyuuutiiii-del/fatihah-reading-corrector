PRIVATE REQUEST

Request Marriage or Family Support

Your request is intended for private verification and matching with an appropriate facilitator, counsellor, mediator or support person. It is not published as a public family dispute or dating profile.

Safety first: this should not be handled as an ordinary counselling or reconciliation request. Use appropriate local emergency, safeguarding, healthcare or security help and a trusted local institution.
Private contact
Your contact is stored privately for verification and matching. It should not be shown in a public family or marriage listing.

Confidentiality limit: Muslim Welfare should protect privacy, but a qualified helper may still need to escalate immediate safeguarding risks according to applicable local responsibilities.
