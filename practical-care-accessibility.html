♿ Accessibility Assistance

Reading, carrying, navigating, errands and other non-clinical help for a Muslim with an accessibility need.

What this service entails

These are the main forms of practical help normally covered under this service.

Reading and document assistance

A volunteer may read ordinary text aloud, help locate important information, or explain simple written material while respecting confidentiality.

Navigation and accompaniment

A helper may accompany the person through a mosque, clinic, public building or community programme when physical or visual navigation is difficult.

Errands and carrying assistance

A volunteer may help collect agreed essentials, carry manageable items or complete simple practical tasks that are difficult because of an accessibility need.

Communication support

Where possible, a suitable volunteer may help the person communicate practical needs to a mosque, organiser or service provider without taking over the person’s decisions.

Connection to specialised support

If the need requires equipment, clinical care, sign-language expertise or another specialised service, FARD should help connect the person to an appropriately qualified provider.

Safety and boundaries

Verify the need
A mosque, organisation or FARD moderator can confirm the local context before matching.
Protect privacy
Exact address and private contact details stay hidden until a suitable match is accepted.
Keep the role practical
This is community assistance and does not replace emergency, medical, legal or other professional services.
