Why keep both names?

Muslim Welfare is the easy public-facing name. FARD remains the deeper verified service and matching network behind many welfare actions.

This means Muslim Welfare can grow as its own app without losing the work already built inside FARD.

Worldwide design principles

Easy language
Ordinary Muslims should understand the service immediately without needing specialist terminology.
Private by default
Family, financial and marriage matters should not become public profiles or public disputes.
Local verification
Mosques and recognised organisations can help verify people and genuine needs.
Qualified escalation
Religious rulings, serious family conflict, legal matters and safeguarding issues go to appropriately qualified people.
