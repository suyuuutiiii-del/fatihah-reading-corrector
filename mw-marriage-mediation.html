⚖️ Marital Dispute Mediation

Neutral, private help for ordinary marital disputes where both spouses freely agree to mediation.

What this service entails

Define the dispute

The mediator identifies the specific issues requiring agreement instead of allowing every past grievance to dominate the meeting.

Hear both sides fairly

Each spouse has time to explain their position without interruption, abuse or intimidation.

Identify common interests

The mediator looks for shared goals such as children’s welfare, household stability, finances or respectful family relations.

Develop possible agreements

The spouses explore realistic options and consequences rather than being forced into one predetermined outcome.

Write down agreed points

Where useful, practical agreements can be recorded so both spouses remember what was decided.

Escalate difficult cases appropriately

Questions involving rights, divorce procedure, custody, property or binding religious rulings should go to qualified scholars or legal professionals as appropriate.

Important: Mediation is voluntary. Violence, threats, coercive control or serious safeguarding concerns require a safety-focused response rather than ordinary mediation.
