📚 Marriage Preparation

Structured preparation before nikāḥ so important expectations are discussed before they become major conflicts.

What this service entails

Faith and values

Discuss worship, religious practice, lifestyle expectations and how differences will be handled respectfully.

Financial expectations

Discuss income, spending, debt, mahr, household expenses, saving and financial responsibilities before marriage.

Living arrangements

Clarify where the couple expects to live, privacy needs and the role of extended family in the home.

Communication habits

Discuss how disagreements, apologies, difficult conversations and decision-making will normally be handled.

Children and parenting

Discuss hopes regarding children, parenting roles, education and important family values.

Health and important disclosures

Important matters that could materially affect married life should be handled honestly and privately, with professional guidance where appropriate.

Family boundaries

Clarify respectful involvement of parents and relatives so neither spouse feels abandoned or controlled.

Important: Marriage preparation is education and structured discussion; complex fiqh, medical or legal questions should be referred to appropriately qualified people.
