Important distinctions

Loan
Money is received now and the same agreed principal is expected back. FARD should not add interest, profit or hidden fees.
Borrowed item
An item is temporarily used and then returned in the agreed condition and time.
Gift
The giver transfers money or an item freely. The receiver does not owe repayment merely because it was given.
Zakah
Zakah should not be treated as an ordinary gift automatically. Eligibility and the relevant zakah rules should be checked separately.

FARD boundaries

No interest or profit from a simple qard
The branch is for interest-free community assistance, not commercial lending.
FARD does not hold the money
The platform matches and records; it should not become a bank or wallet.
No public debt shaming
Repayment difficulty should be handled privately and respectfully.
Write down clear terms
Amount/item, date, return condition and agreed repayment/return plan should be recorded before hand-off.
