🤝 Marriage Introduction

Private, verified adult introductions for Muslims genuinely seeking marriage.

What this service entails

Confidential registration

The person gives only the information needed for a verified facilitator to understand the request; there is no public searchable profile wall.

Identity and seriousness check

A facilitator or recognised organisation can confirm that the person is an adult and is genuinely seeking marriage rather than casual or secret relationships.

Basic compatibility information

Important non-sensitive matters such as location, age range, family situation, education/work expectations and broad marriage goals can be discussed privately.

Mutual consent before contact

Neither party receives the other person’s private contact information until both have agreed to an introduction.

Family or walī involvement

Where appropriate to the people involved and the applicable Islamic practice, a family member or walī can be included early rather than after a secret relationship has developed.

Safe first communication

Early communication should use an agreed, respectful setting and avoid pressure, secrecy or unnecessary emotional dependency.

Important: This service is for adults seeking marriage. It is not a public dating system, and it should not expose photos, phone numbers or personal histories to strangers.
