🌿 Post-Separation Family Support

Practical welfare support when reconciliation is not possible or a couple has already separated.

What this service entails

Respectful communication boundaries

Help establish practical methods and times for necessary communication so conflict does not continue constantly.

Children’s welfare

Keep children out of adult hostility and focus on stable routines, care and appropriate contact arrangements.

Practical household transition

Support may include temporary housing referrals, transport, essential items or other welfare needs without exposing the family publicly.

Religious and legal referral

Questions about divorce procedure, waiting periods, custody, maintenance or property should be referred to qualified scholars and local legal professionals as appropriate.

Emotional and community support

Trusted family, community or qualified counselling support can reduce isolation during a difficult transition.

Avoid public shaming

Neither former spouse should be turned into public gossip or a community spectacle through the welfare platform.

Important: If there is abuse, stalking, threats or immediate danger, safeguarding and appropriate local professional help take priority.
