Smart Equalize is the mode to use when one page looks smaller, thinner, larger, or has much more white margin than another. It preserves the page's proportions and centres the content instead of stretching it.

Important note

To make visible content margins truly uniform across mixed PDFs, this tool renders each page and rebuilds the corrected PDF. This is reliable for scanned/image-based books and mixed page sizes, but selectable text, links, forms and some PDF annotations may become flattened in the output. Keep the original PDF as your master copy.
