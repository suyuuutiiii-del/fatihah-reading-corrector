💍 Marriage & Family Welfare

Private, respectful support from the stage of seeking a spouse through preparation, married life, conflict resolution and, where necessary, safe post-separation support.

🤝 Marriage Introduction

Verified adult introductions for Muslims genuinely seeking marriage, with privacy, consent and family/walī involvement where appropriate.

📚 Marriage Preparation

Discuss expectations, responsibilities, finances, communication, family boundaries and important questions before nikāḥ.

🫶 Marriage Counselling

Structured help with communication, recurring tension, unmet expectations and rebuilding cooperation.

⚖️ Dispute Mediation

Neutral, private mediation for ordinary marital disagreements where both spouses freely agree to participate.

Open →

🕊️ Divorce Prevention & Reconciliation

Early intervention to identify solvable problems, reduce escalation and explore reconciliation when it is safe and genuinely voluntary.

Open →

🌿 Post-Separation Family Support

Practical support for respectful separation, children’s welfare, communication boundaries and referral for legal or scholarly questions.

Safety boundary: abuse, violence, threats, coercive control or immediate danger should not be treated as an ordinary “marriage dispute” requiring reconciliation. Safety, safeguarding and appropriate professional/local help come first.
