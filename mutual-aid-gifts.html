🎁 Gifts & Sadaqah

Money or useful items given freely to a verified person or household with no repayment or return expected.

What this service entails

Gift money

A giver may privately give a modest amount to a verified person or household without creating a debt.

Gift useful items

Clothing, food, books, household goods, school items or other lawful useful property may be given permanently.

Anonymous or quiet giving

Where practical, the giver may choose not to have their identity displayed publicly, while FARD still keeps enough information for moderation.

Verified recipient need

A mosque, organisation or moderator may confirm the situation so gifts are directed responsibly without humiliating the recipient.

No repayment expected

A genuine gift should not later be converted into a debt, favour or pressure unless both parties clearly made a different agreement beforehand.

Separate zakah handling

If the giver specifically intends zakah, the recipient’s eligibility and applicable zakah rules should be checked separately rather than assuming every gift qualifies.
