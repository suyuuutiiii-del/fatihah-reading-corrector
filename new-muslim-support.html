🌱 New Muslim Support

Help a new Muslim find a trustworthy mentor, a welcoming mosque, basic learning support and a community that does not overwhelm or exploit them.

What this service can cover

🤝 Mentorship

A steady trustworthy person who can answer basic questions and help the new Muslim settle.

🕌 Mosque welcome

Introduce the person to a suitable local mosque without pressure or embarrassment.

🧎 Worship basics

Simple support for wuḍūʾ, ṣalāh and other foundational practices.

📖 Learning

Gradual Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic learning matched to the person's level.

🌙 Ramadan

Practical fasting, ifṭār and mosque support during the first Ramadans.

🫶 Belonging

Help reduce isolation by connecting the person to safe, welcoming Muslim community life.

What you can do

New Muslim Support safety rules

No coercion
A mentor must not control, pressure or shame a new Muslim into unnecessary commitments.
No financial exploitation
Mentoring must not become a route to loans, sales, fundraising pressure or dependence.
Respect scholarly differences
Basic support should not weaponise legitimate fiqh differences or demand sectarian loyalty.
Gender and meeting safeguards
Use appropriate same-gender mentoring or safe group/public arrangements where appropriate.
Protect vulnerable disclosures
Family conflict, immigration status, trauma and other sensitive matters should not be posted publicly.
Escalate specialist issues
Complex legal, mental-health, safeguarding or personal religious questions should go to qualified professionals or scholars.
