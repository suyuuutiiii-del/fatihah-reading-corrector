🎁 Gifts & Sadaqah

Money or useful items given freely to a verified person or household with no repayment or return expected.

What this service entails

Gift money

A giver may privately give a modest amount to a verified person or household without creating a debt.

Gift useful items

Clothing, food, books, household goods, school items or other useful property may be given permanently.

Quiet giving

Where practical, the giver can keep their identity from public display while FARD retains enough information for moderation.

Verify genuine need

A mosque, organisation or moderator may confirm the situation so giving is responsible without humiliating the recipient.

No repayment expected

A genuine gift should not later be converted into a debt, favour or pressure.

Keep zakah distinct

If the giver intends zakah, eligibility and the relevant zakah rules should be checked separately.

Important safeguards

No strings attached. Gifts should not become pressure for personal favours, business, political activity or unwanted relationships.
Protect dignity. Recipients should not be photographed or publicly displayed merely because they received help.
